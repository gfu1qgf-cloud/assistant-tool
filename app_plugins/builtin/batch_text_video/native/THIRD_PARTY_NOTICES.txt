Rebuild on Windows x64 with Rust 1.99.1 GNU:
cargo build --locked --release --target x86_64-pc-windows-gnu
The tested build used bundled rust-lld.exe as linker with RUSTFLAGS:
-C linker-flavor=ld.lld -C link-self-contained=yes
No Rust installation, DaVinci Resolve, Avid SDK, or network is needed at runtime.
